## en
Doxycycline is a prescription antibiotic that is also used for acne and rosacea. The NHS advises swallowing it with a glass of water while sitting or standing, and not lying down for at least 30 minutes afterwards to avoid throat irritation. Antacids and iron or zinc supplements may not mix well with it, so check timing with a pharmacist, and it is best to avoid alcohol because it can stop the medicine working properly. It can make your skin sensitive to sunlight, so tell a healthcare professional about rash, itching or redness after sun exposure. It is not usually used in pregnancy or breastfeeding and needs a doctor's check if you have liver or kidney problems; get medical help straight away for bloody diarrhoea or trouble breathing.

## zh-Hans
多西环素是一种需要处方的抗生素，也用于治疗痤疮和玫瑰痤疮。NHS建议坐着或站着用一杯水吞服，服药后至少30分钟内不要躺下，以免刺激喉咙。抗酸药以及铁、锌补充剂可能会影响它，请向药师确认间隔时间；酒精可能使药效降低，最好避免饮酒。它可能使皮肤对阳光敏感，晒太阳后如果出现皮疹、瘙痒或发红，请告知医务人员。孕期和哺乳期一般不使用，有肝肾疾病需先咨询医生；如出现带血腹泻或呼吸困难，请立即就医。
