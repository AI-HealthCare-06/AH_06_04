## en
The American Academy of Dermatology (AAD) describes what follows laser hair removal. Right after treatment the skin is often red and swollen, like a mild sunburn; this discomfort, swelling and redness usually lasts 1 to 3 days and there is generally no downtime. Apply a cool compress to ease discomfort, keep direct sunlight off the treated skin, and avoid tanning beds and sun lamps. Hair is reduced by about 10% to 25% after the first treatment; most people need 2 to 6 treatments, usually once every 4 to 6 weeks, but your clinician decides when the next one is safe. Maintenance treatments may be needed later. Blistering, cold sore outbreaks, infection, scarring and skin lightening or darkening are rare but possible; colour usually returns to normal over time, though some changes can be permanent. Follow your clinic's aftercare instructions and ask them if anything concerns you.

## zh-Hans
